# Ariadns Domain Glossary (Ubiquitous Language)

### DomainL1
Initial root domain under investigation (Depth 1 target).

### DomainL2
Associated domain discovered via pivoting from DomainL1 (Depth 2 target).

### CandidateOriginIP
Historical pre-CDN IPv4/IPv6 address or non-CDN subdomain IP candidate for origin verification.

### PropositionNode
Atomic epistemological unit in the causal evidence chain, categorized as either an observed Fact or a derived Conclusion.

### DataCustodian
Legal and regulatory disclosure authority mapped deterministically from an actionable Conclusion node.

### EvidenceChainDAG
Strictly acyclic directed causal graph where edges denote logical derivation from upstream premises to downstream conclusions.

### SessionAuditEntry
Immutable stepwise execution record capturing timestamp, module, target, added facts, and cryptographic state digest.

### NextStepRecommendation
Rule-derived investigative action inferred from existing propositions and past audit history.

### ArtifactManifest
Dual-layer cryptographic manifest asserting bit-for-bit forensic integrity across all generated case artifacts.

### Workbench
Local web-based operator console for progressive OSINT case orchestration, interactive evidence DAG inspection, and dual-layer verification.
